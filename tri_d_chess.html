♛ Tri-D Chess

tri-level 3D chess · click a piece, then click where it goes
How to play. Click one of your pieces — its legal destinations light up (dots = move, rings = capture). Click a highlighted square to move. Pieces are 3D: rooks slide along any axis, bishops on planar diagonals, queens both, a true 3D knight, the king one step. Attack boards (the small 2×2 platforms at the corners): slide a piece onto one, then use the → move chips beneath it to fly the whole platform (carrying its piece) to an adjacent pin — flip front/back or change level. Checkmate the enemy king.
