Privacy

Last updated: November 25, 2026.

The GOAT Gamble games (Chess, Connect Four and Blackjack) run entirely in your browser. They have no accounts, no ads, no cookies and no analytics, and they don't send your data anywhere. Nothing is stored after you close the page.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Neon Dash is a separate game with its own privacy policy.
